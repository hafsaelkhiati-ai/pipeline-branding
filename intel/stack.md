# Stack

## Business / Initiative
Pipeline Branding — a system that generates the brand strategy for each project (service or product): checks if the name is free (domain + rough trademark exposure), designs a logo, lays out a business card, lays out a flyer, and keeps everything visually consistent — replacing the manual, one-brand-at-a-time process currently used.

## Products / Services / Responsibilities
- **Brand Strategy Pipeline** — automated sequence covering name/domain/trademark clearance check, logo design, business card layout, flyer layout, and visual consistency enforcement across all brand assets for a client project.
