Concept A: "Der Weg an der Lauter"

A road that is also the Lauter river, winding up to a warm destination point. Calm blue for trust and safety, teal for care, amber for the warm arrival. Says: we get you there safely, and we are from here.

Concept B: "Sitzend sicher"

An "L" drawn as a road, which also reads as a seated passenger (head, back, lap). That is exactly what a Krankenfahrt is: seated, accompanied transport. Deep navy for reliability, amber for warmth and humanity.
