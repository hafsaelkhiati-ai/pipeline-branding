# Sub-Blueprint: 05 — Business Card

*Part of [pipeline-brand-strategy.md](../pipeline-brand-strategy.md). Read the global orchestrator first.*

**Status:** Draft v0.1 — unvalidated until the MedPfalz Mobil run confirms it.

## Goal

Lay out a business card using the approved logo and the brand's contact/service info.

## Inputs

- Approved logo (from Stage 4)
- Contact info (phone, email, address) from intake
- Tagline
- Brand colors/fonts established during the logo stage

## Steps

1. Draft card copy (name, tagline, contact info, key service line if it fits) using ChatGPT.
2. Lay out the card in Canva using the approved logo, brand colors, and drafted copy.
3. Produce a front/back design if the card needs both sides (e.g. tagline/services on the back).
4. Present the draft to Hafsa for approval before it's considered final.

## Decision Point

Hafsa approves the card layout before it's added to the final client package. If contact info is incomplete (as with MedPfalz Mobil pending the client's real name/domain/email), stop at a draft stage and flag what's missing rather than inventing placeholder contact details.

## Output

Business card file(s), front and back if applicable.

## Equipment

None yet.

- **Print specs** — exact dimensions, bleed, and file format (e.g. standard 85×55mm vs US 3.5×2in, PDF vs PNG export). Left open; gets settled on the first real run and then locked in as the standing default for future projects.
