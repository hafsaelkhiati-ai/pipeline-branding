# Sub-Blueprint: 06 — Flyer

*Part of [pipeline-brand-strategy.md](../pipeline-brand-strategy.md). Read the global orchestrator first.*

**Status:** Draft v0.1 — unvalidated until the MedPfalz Mobil run confirms it.

## Goal

Lay out a flyer using the approved logo, brand assets, and the business's service list.

## Inputs

- Approved logo (from Stage 4)
- Services list, Tagline, Target Audience, Contact info from intake
- Brand colors/fonts established during the logo stage

## Steps

1. Draft flyer copy using ChatGPT: headline/tagline, the services list in scannable form, a call-to-action, contact info.
2. Lay out the flyer in Canva using the approved logo, brand colors, and drafted copy.
3. Present the draft to Hafsa for approval before it's considered final.

## Decision Point

Hafsa approves the flyer before it's added to the final client package. If any required content (services, contact info) is incomplete, stop and flag what's missing rather than inventing placeholder content.

## Output

Flyer file, ready for print or digital distribution as needed.

## Equipment

None yet.

- **Print/format specs** — exact size (e.g. A5, A4, US Letter), orientation, and file format. Left open; gets settled on the first real run and then locked in as the standing default for future projects.
